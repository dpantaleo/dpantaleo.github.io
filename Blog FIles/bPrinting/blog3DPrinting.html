Background

I bought a 3D printer to make sets of cosplay armour because I am a nerd. The first set I ventured to create was the B-01 Tactical armour from Helldivers 2. This armour set has a good mix of hard parts (the armour plates) and soft parts (cape and coat), and a fan model is available online.

Design

The Helldivers 2 files were obtained from Galactic Armory, "Arming the Galaxy Since 2017". Find their models at galacticarmory.net.

As time goes on and I create my own models, more detail will be shared here and on the CAD Modelling page of this site.

Lessons Learned the Hard Way

Fun fact: the default Bambu lab ABS settings are not good.

*img of horrific failure*

Well maybe they're ok but this specific part of the back plate is a killer to print. This piece of crap took me four separate tries to print properly, with each attempt thwarted by a different kind of print failure.

First, a stringing failure caused by the print losing adhesion to the print bed.

Second, the spool runs out of plastic mid-print but gets stuck in the feed mechanism so the printer doesn't detect it isn't depositing anything.

Third, despite changing temperature parameters it decides to string anyway.

Attempt number four: Perfection (still yet to happen).

Raw Prints

Due to my printing using the default Bambu labs ABS settings, some of my early parts have significant warping and do not line up with each other correctly.

Sanding and Joining

Sanding is pretty straightforward.

For joining separate parts of one plate, I originally tried a slow-drying superglue, before immediately getting enraged at how annoying it was and instead using a soldering iron to melt the two parts together.
