Background

I recently moved to a house within walkable distance from Ruthven Station on the Mernda line. Rather than check the PTV app on my phone to see when the next city-bound train is, I figured I could program an LED display using a microcontroller to display departure times using the publicly available PTV Timetable API. This could potentially save me a whopping 10 seconds to take out my phone and check the app.
